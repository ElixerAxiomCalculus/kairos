# Catering Services Guide

## 1. Catering availability
In-house catering is available at Banyan Hall and Skyline Terrace through Saffron Kitchen, the Deccan Conference Centre's catering partner. Lotus Room is limited to beverages and light snacks. External caterers are not permitted at the Deccan Conference Centre.

## 2. Menus and dietary options
Vegetarian, vegan, Jain and gluten-free meals are available on request with 5 working days' notice. The standard lunch buffet costs ₹850 per person.

## 3. Minimum numbers
A buffet requires a minimum of 20 guests. For groups smaller than 20, packed meals are provided instead of a buffet.

## 4. Catering for events

### 4.1 Standard events
For standard events, final catering numbers must be confirmed 3 working days before the event.

### 4.2 International events
For international events, menus must be confirmed 10 working days before the event. Continental and pan-Asian menus are available for international delegations, and halal-certified meals can be arranged on request.

## 5. Alcohol
Alcohol is not served at Company venues.
