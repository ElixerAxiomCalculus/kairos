# Venue Booking Terms

## 1. Scope
These terms apply to every booking of a Company venue, including the Deccan Conference Centre in Pune and Harbourview Suites in Mumbai.

## 2. Booking procedure
Venue requests must be submitted through the Facilities portal at least 10 working days before the event. A booking is confirmed only after a deposit of 25% of the venue fee has been paid. The booking owner receives a confirmation number by email.

## 3. Cancellation policy

### 3.1 Standard events
Cancellations made 14 or more days before a standard event are free of charge. Cancellations made 7 to 13 days before the event incur a fee of 50% of the venue fee. Cancellations made less than 7 days before the event incur the full venue fee.

### 3.2 International events
For international events, cancellations must be made at least 30 days before the event to receive a refund of the venue fee. The 25% deposit for an international event is non-refundable in all cases. Cancellations made less than 30 days before an international event incur 75% of the venue fee.

## 4. Changes to bookings
The expected headcount can be changed up to 72 hours before the event without a fee. Room changes are subject to availability and must be requested through the Facilities portal.

## 5. Payment
The remaining venue fee is invoiced to the booking owner's cost centre within 7 days after the event.
