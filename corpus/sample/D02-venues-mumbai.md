# Mumbai Venue Catalogue

## 1. Overview
Harbourview Suites in Lower Parel, Mumbai is the Company's venue for events in Mumbai. It offers two bookable spaces: Marine Hall and Pearl Room.

## 2. Marine Hall
Marine Hall seats up to 120 participants in a theatre layout and up to 70 participants in a classroom layout. It includes a stage, two projectors and a wireless microphone kit.

## 3. Pearl Room
Pearl Room is a meeting room that seats up to 16 participants. It is intended for small working sessions and interviews.

## 4. Access
Harbourview Suites is a five-minute walk from Lower Parel railway station. Paid parking is available in the building basement.
