# Venue Booking FAQ (2023 edition)

## 1. How far ahead should I book?
Book your venue as early as possible. Popular rooms are often reserved several weeks in advance.

## 2. Can I change the headcount after booking?
Yes. The expected headcount can be changed up to 24 hours before the event without a fee.

## 3. Who do I contact about a booking?
Contact the Facilities team through the Facilities portal. For urgent issues on the day of the event, speak to the venue reception.
