# Event Security and Guest Registration

## 1. Visitor registration
Every external guest must be registered in the visitor system before arriving at a Company venue. Guests receive a temporary badge at reception on presentation of photo identification.

## 2. Guest lists for events

### 2.1 Standard events
For standard events, the guest list must be submitted to Security 2 working days before the event.

### 2.2 International events
For international events, the guest list with passport details must be submitted to Security 21 days before the event. Foreign nationals must carry their passport on the day of the event. Security will provide a short briefing to the event owner.

## 3. Restricted areas
Guests may not enter server rooms, laboratories or other restricted areas, even when escorted.
