# Pune Venue Catalogue

## 1. Overview
The Deccan Conference Centre in Baner, Pune is the Company's primary venue for workshops, trainings and offsites in Pune. It offers three bookable spaces: Banyan Hall, Lotus Room and Skyline Terrace. All spaces are booked through the Facilities portal.

## 2. Banyan Hall
Banyan Hall seats up to 40 participants in a classroom layout and up to 60 participants in a theatre layout. It is suited to workshops of 20 to 40 people. The hall has a ceiling-mounted projector, two whiteboards and a ceiling microphone array. Banyan Hall is wheelchair accessible via the ground-floor entrance.

## 3. Lotus Room
Lotus Room is a boardroom that seats up to 24 participants around a single table. It has a video-conferencing system with a 75-inch display. Lotus Room is not suitable for groups larger than 24 people.

## 4. Skyline Terrace
Skyline Terrace is an open-air rooftop space that holds up to 80 people standing or 50 people seated. It is suited to networking sessions and evening receptions. Skyline Terrace is not available from June to September because of the monsoon season.

## 5. Parking and access
The Deccan Conference Centre has parking for 30 cars. The nearest metro station is Baner Road, a ten-minute walk from the venue. Visitors must collect a badge from the ground-floor reception.
