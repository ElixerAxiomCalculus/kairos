# AV and IT Support for Events

## 1. Standard equipment
All Company venues provide a projector or display, an HDMI connection and guest Wi-Fi. Wireless microphones are available on request.

## 2. Requesting technical support
On-site technical support must be requested through the IT service desk at least 3 working days before the event. A technician is present for the first 30 minutes of the event.

## 3. Hybrid events
A hybrid streaming kit with a camera, a speaker microphone and a streaming laptop can be reserved for events with remote participants. The kit must be reserved 5 working days in advance.
