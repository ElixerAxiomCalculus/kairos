# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy covers reimbursement of business travel and related expenses for all employees.

## 2. Submitting claims
Expense claims are submitted through the expense portal and approved by the employee's line manager. An itemised receipt is required for every expense above ₹500.

## 3. Travel

### 3.1 Domestic travel
Domestic flights are reimbursed at economy class. Claims for domestic travel must be submitted within 30 days of return. The domestic per diem for meals and incidentals is ₹2,500 per day, and hotel stays are reimbursed up to ₹6,000 per night.

### 3.2 International travel
International travel requires pre-approval from the department head at least 21 days before departure. International flights under 8 hours are reimbursed at economy class, and flights of 8 hours or more at premium economy. Claims for international travel must be submitted within 45 days of return. The international per diem follows the country schedule, with a default of US$75 per day. Company-arranged travel insurance is mandatory for international trips.

## 4. Non-reimbursable expenses
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines and personally chosen upgrades are not reimbursed.

## 5. Payment timeline
Approved claims are paid to the employee's salary account within 10 working days of approval.
